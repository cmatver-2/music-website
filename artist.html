Biography

Luna Rivers is a Kerala-based indie artist known for her dreamy Lo-Fi compositions that blend traditional South Indian melodies with modern electronic production. Starting her musical journey at age 12 with classical Carnatic training, she later discovered electronic music and developed a unique sound that resonates deeply with listeners seeking calm in a noisy world.

Her debut EP Monsoon Moods (2022) gained over 200K streams on independent platforms within its first month. She performs regularly at cafes and art spaces across Kochi and Trivandrum, and collaborates with local visual artists to create immersive audiovisual experiences.
