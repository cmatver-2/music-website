Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

🔒 Privacy Policy Last Updated: June 2026

1 Introduction

Welcome to BeatWave. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be encountered when you use our music player website, how it is used, and what steps are taken to protect it. BeatWave is a personal educational project — not a commercial service — and operates with that in mind.

This website does not require you to create an account, log in, or submit any personal information to use its core features. You can browse, listen, and explore without identifying yourself in any way.

2 Information We Collect

BeatWave does not intentionally collect personal information. However, certain technical information may be incidentally processed:

  • IP address (via web server or hosting provider logs)
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited and time of access
  • Referring URL (the page you came from)

This information is a standard by-product of how the internet works and is not actively gathered or stored by the project itself.

3 Local Browser Storage

BeatWave uses your browser's localStorage to remember small preferences on your own device — specifically:

  • Your chosen theme (dark or light mode)
  • Songs you have marked as favourites

This data is stored entirely on your own device and is never transmitted to any server. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings (Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data → Cached/local storage).

4 Cookies

BeatWave does not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or analytics cookies. Basic session cookies may be set by the hosting provider as part of standard server operation. No cookies are used to identify or profile individual visitors.

5 How We Use Information

Any incidentally collected information is used solely to:

  • Keep the website operational and accessible
  • Monitor performance and diagnose technical issues
  • Understand general usage patterns to improve the experience
  • Maintain reasonable security

6 Third-Party Services

The website may be hosted using services such as GitHub Pages, Vercel, or Netlify. These providers operate under their own privacy policies and may collect technical data as described above. Additionally:

  • Google Fonts — typography is loaded from Google's servers, which may log your IP address per Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Pixabay — some audio and images are sourced from Pixabay under their content licence. Direct links to their platform may be subject to Pixabay's Privacy Policy.

7 Media & Copyright

Music tracks and artist images on this website are sourced from Pixabay and used in accordance with Pixabay's content licence. All copyright and ownership rights remain with the respective creators and rights holders. This project makes no claim of ownership over third-party media.

8 Data Security

Reasonable care is taken to keep the website secure. However, no method of internet transmission is completely secure. Because this site does not collect personal data, the risk to users is minimal.

9 Children's Privacy

This website is not directed toward children under 13 years of age and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

10 Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised date at the top. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
